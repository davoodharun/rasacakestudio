Meet Rasa Cake Studio

I’m a Sri Lankan American baker who grew up around the smell of cardamom, coconut, and jaggery. When I moved to the States, I brought those flavors with me and started turning them into cakes that feel like home.

Every cake here is made by hand in small batches — no shortcuts, no mass production. Whether you want something classic, something with a Sri Lankan twist, or a design that’s entirely yours, we’ll make it happen.
